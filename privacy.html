Privacy

Your information

We keep this simple. We collect only what you send us: your name, email, phone number and business details. We use it to reply to you and to deliver the service you sign up for.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not text your customers.
  • This site has no tracking scripts or ads.

Your customers' details

If you're a client, we use the customer list you give us only to send your review request emails. Every email includes a way to opt out, and we honour opt-outs.

Deleting your information

Ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing grandriverreviews@gmail.com.
