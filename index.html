FAQ

Questions trades owners ask us

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month. Cancel anytime by email and we stop at the end of your current paid month.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile, which takes about two minutes. You can remove us any time.

What happens when someone leaves a bad review?

We write a calm, professional reply and send it to you first. It only goes up once you've OK'd it. Positive and neutral reviews are answered directly, within one business day.

Will you write reviews for my business?

No, never. Fake reviews break Google's rules and can get a profile suspended. Every review you get comes from a real customer.

How do you ask my customers for reviews?

Once a month we send a short, friendly email to the recent customers on the list you give us. We ask all of them, and every email has a clear way to opt out. We don't send texts.

What do I need to send you?

A list of recent customers' names and emails each month, plus any job photos you're happy to share for posts. That's it.

Can you guarantee more calls or a higher rating?

No one honestly can. What we guarantee is the work: every review answered, four posts a month, and review requests going out. Results vary.

Do you only work with roofers and HVAC?

That's where we started, but we're happy to talk with any local trade in Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge or Guelph.
